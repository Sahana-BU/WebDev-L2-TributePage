HER JOURNEY

About Mother Teresa

Mother Teresa was a Catholic nun and humanitarian known for helping poor, sick, and homeless people. She was born on 26 August 1910 in Skopje, which is now in North Macedonia.

She moved to India and devoted much of her life to serving people who were suffering. In 1950, she founded the Missionaries of Charity in Calcutta, now known as Kolkata.

Her life reminds us that even small acts of kindness can bring hope and comfort to others.
